What information we collect

We collect the details you give us and the messages you send us.

  • Contact and enquiry forms: your name, email, phone number, course of interest and message.
  • Facebook and Instagram lead forms: the details you submit, plus the ad or form you responded to.
  • WhatsApp: your phone number, WhatsApp profile name, messages and any files you send us.
  • Website chat assistant: the questions you type.
  • Student details: grade, board, subjects and similar information shared when you enquire or enrol.
  • Calls and follow-ups: notes our team records about your enquiry.

Our website does not currently use advertising cookies. The Google map on our contact page may set its own cookies.

How we use information

We use your information to answer enquiries and provide tuition.

  • Reply to your questions by phone, WhatsApp or email
  • Arrange trial classes, enrolment and schedules
  • Follow up on enquiries you started
  • Send updates or offers where you have agreed to receive them
  • Keep records and meet our legal obligations

We rely on your consent, on steps needed before or under an enrolment, and on our legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information.

WhatsApp messaging

Messages you send to +971 58 575 5286 are received by our team and stored in our customer management system. We use the WhatsApp Business Platform provided by Meta.

  • Your enquiry may be saved as a lead so our team can follow up.
  • Recent chat history on this number may be imported into that system.

Students under 18

Many of our students are under 18, so we expect a parent or guardian to make enquiries and enrol them. A parent or guardian can ask to see, correct or delete their child's information at any time. If you believe a child has given us information without a parent's knowledge, contact us and we will review and remove it where appropriate.

Storage, security and transfers

We keep information only as long as we need it for the purposes above or as the law requires. Access to our customer management system is limited to authorised staff, and integration credentials are stored encrypted.

Some providers process data outside the UAE. Where this happens, we rely on the safeguards those providers offer and on applicable UAE data protection law.

Your rights and deleting your data

Subject to UAE law, including the Personal Data Protection Law, you can ask us to:

  • Give you a copy of your information
  • Correct it
  • Delete it
  • Limit or stop how we use it
  • Withdraw consent you gave

To request deletion, email aurafirstscience@gmail.com or message +971 58 575 5286 on WhatsApp with your name and phone number. Write "Delete my data" in your message. We may need to confirm your identity, and we may keep records the law requires.
